INTERNAL ARCHITECTURE

ENGINEERED WITH PRECISION

An automotive engineering cross-section revealing the advanced material composition of our lead-acid and tubular energy storage cells.

01

Solid Copper Terminals & Magic Eye Optical Hydrometer

Heavy forged lead-copper posts prevent terminal melting, while the 3-state optical hydrometer provides instant charge diagnostics.

02

Ultra Polypropylene Lid with Ceramic Flame Arrestors

Heat-welded labyrinth lid channels escaping electrolyte back into cells while porous ceramic discs prevent external sparks.

03

Positive Calcium-Tin-Silver Spine Plate

High-pressure die-cast spines ensure void-free crystalline structure for extreme corrosion resistance under deep cycling.

04

Enveloped Polyethylene (PE) Pocket Separator

Microporous envelope completely encloses positive plates to eliminate bottom dendrite growth and short-circuit failure.

05

High Acid Volume Electrolyte Reservoir

Generous headroom provides extra acid volume that serves as an internal coolant, keeping grid temperatures stable up to 50°C.

06

Through-Partition Short-Path Intercell Welding

Computerized ultrasonic through-wall resistance welds offer near-zero voltage drop for explosive cold cranking burst amps.
